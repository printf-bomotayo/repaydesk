# Collections policy (Demo Microfinance Bank)

How the bank asks for late repayments: what may be said, to whom, when and how often. Every message
the drafting agent writes is checked against the rules marked **[checked]** in code
(`repaydesk.collections.policy`) before a person sees it, and nothing is sent until a portfolio
manager approves it. The rest the drafting agent is told, and the manager judges.

This is the demo bank's own policy, written to be firm and fair. A real bank would align it with
its regulator's and the consumer protection authority's rules on debt recovery.

## Who we contact

- **Only the borrower**, at the phone number on their file. **[checked]** Never a guarantor, a
  relative, an employer, or anyone in their contacts, and never in a group.
- A loan more than **90 days late** goes to the recovery team, people, not messages. The agent
  drafts nothing for it. **[checked]**
- A borrower with an **open promise to pay** that isn't due yet is left alone until the promised
  date. **[checked]**

## When

- Between **08:00 and 18:00**, Lagos time, **Monday to Saturday**. Never on Sunday or a public
  holiday. **[checked]**
- At most **two messages per loan in any seven days.** **[checked]**

## What every message says

- The borrower's first name, and the bank's name.
- The **loan reference** and the **amount now due**, exactly as core banking has it. **[checked]**
- **How to pay**: the loan's virtual account, or any branch with the loan reference.
- **An offer to talk**: reply to the message, or call the branch.

## How it says it, by how late

| Days late | Bucket | Tone | Asks for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 to 7 | Reminder | Friendly; it may be an oversight | Payment |
| 8 to 30 | Follow-up | Firm and polite | Payment, or a date they will pay (a promise) |
| 31 to 90 | Arrangement | Serious, supportive | A conversation, to agree a plan |
| Over 90 | Recovery | (a person handles it) | |
| A promise broken | Follow-up | Firm; refers to the promised date | A new date, or a conversation |

## What it never says

**[checked]** No threats, and nothing that shames or frightens:

- no police, arrest, prison, court, lawyers, the EFCC;
- no "your family", "your contacts" or "everyone will know";
- no "blacklist", and no credit bureau before a person decides it;
- nothing in capitals for emphasis, and no more than one exclamation mark.

Also: no links (people should never be taught to click a link in a message about money), no
requests for a PIN, password, BVN or card number. **[checked]**

## Length

- SMS: at most **320 characters** (two segments). **[checked]**
- WhatsApp: at most **600 characters**. **[checked]**

## After a message

- A reply with a date ("I go pay Friday") becomes a **promise to pay**: the amount and date go on
  the loan, and the borrower isn't messaged again before then.
- A promise is **kept** when a repayment of at least the promised amount posts by the promised date,
  and **broken** otherwise. A broken promise brings the loan back to the morning's list.
